## 2. Which photos Virustra needs

| Website placement | Photo to create | Recommended shape | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Model wearing a statement outfit in an elegant setting | Wide landscape, around 16:9 | Premium first impression |
| Mobile hero | Model and outfit framed vertically | Portrait, around 4:5 or 3:4 | Keep face and outfit visible on phones |

| Embroidery detail | Close-up of stitching, sequins, borders and fabric | Square or portrait | Show craftsmanship and condition |

**Important:** A campaign image should not be the only product image. Customers also need clear photos of the actual garment.

## 3. Virustra's recommended photography style

Use **Royal Editorial** as the overall direction:

- **Colours:** deep maroon, oxblood, antique gold, warm ivory and muted earthy neutrals.
- **Lighting:** soft directional light, warm highlights and controlled shadows; preserve the garment's true colour.
- **Backgrounds:** simple ivory studio, textured neutral wall, heritage-inspired interior or restrained architectural setting.
- **Composition:** mix full-length portraits, three-quarter portraits, fabric movement and close-up details.
- **Styling:** elegant Indian occasionwear; keep jewellery and props secondary to the garment.
- **Consistency:** use similar crops, lighting and background families across product cards.
- **Authenticity:** use Virustra's own available outfits for catalogue photos. Never imply an AI-generated design is available stock.

The goal is **premium and cinematic, but still useful for shopping**. Avoid making every image dark or heavily edited; customers need to judge the real garment.

## 5. Practical photo checklist

Before publishing a product, try to have:

- [ ] Full-length front view.
- [ ] Back view, where relevant.
- [ ] Side or three-quarter view.
- [ ] Close-up of embroidery, fabric and borders.
- [ ] Clear view of blouse/choli details and skirt pairing.
- [ ] Accurate colour under neutral lighting.
- [ ] Size, fit and condition information checked separately.
- [ ] Permission to use the image.
- [ ] Mobile crop checked so important garment details are not cut off.
- [ ] Image compressed for fast loading without visible quality loss.

For the homepage, use one strong hero image rather than a crowded collage. Do not lazy-load the main hero image if it is the page's primary visible image; lazy-load below-the-fold photos.

---

## 6. Copyright and accuracy

- Reference sites are for visual research, not a free image library.
- Do not reuse other brands' photos without permission or a suitable licence.
- Confirm usage rights with the photographer, model and relevant rights holders.
- AI-generated concepts can help with art direction, but must not misrepresent a real product.
- Do not publish invented reviews, stock availability, prices, sizes, delivery promises or rental policies alongside images.
- Preserve Virustra's existing logo as supplied; do not redraw, distort or recolour it unless approved by the brand owner.

---

## 7. Recommended workflow

1. Open the references above and save examples for **pose, lighting, background and crop**.
2. Create a moodboard with three groups: **Hero / Product Catalogue / Occasion Campaigns**.
3. Photograph actual garments first, using consistent lighting and framing.
4. Use concept imagery only for art direction or clearly labelled campaign material.
5. Review photos on desktop and mobile before adding them to the website.

**Best starting combination for Virustra:** Sabyasachi for heritage and richness, Vogue India's Dream Wedding for storytelling, and Behance/ethnic-fashion portfolios for practical poses and shot planning.

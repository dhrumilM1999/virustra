Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is a WhatsApp message a booking?

No. It’s an enquiry. Virustra checks the piece and your date, shares the price and terms, and only then is a rental confirmed.

Can I rent and buy?

Many pieces can be rented, bought, or both. Each piece shows which options it has, with a clearly labelled starting price.

What about deposit, duration and returns?

Rental duration, charges and return rules are confirmed for your piece on WhatsApp before you commit. A full policy page will sit alongside this.

Do you serve my city?

Virustra serves customers across India. Delivery and return timelines depend on your pin code and are confirmed when you enquire.

How do I know it will fit?

Share your size or measurements in the enquiry and we’ll advise on fit. Alteration options are confirmed by Virustra per piece.
